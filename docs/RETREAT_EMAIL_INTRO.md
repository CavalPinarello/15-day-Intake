Hi everyone,

Welcome to the sleep optimization component of the Longevity Retreat (Half Moon Bay, January 16-18).

Over the next **10 days**, you'll use the Zoe Sleep app to complete a comprehensive sleep assessment. This combines clinically-validated questionnaires with your personal sleep data to create a complete picture of your sleep health.

## Quick Setup (5 min)

1. Install **TestFlight** from the App Store
2. Open this link on your iPhone: **https://testflight.apple.com/join/62aRAuVH**
3. Install Zoe Sleep, create an account, complete onboarding (allow HealthKit when prompted)
4. Follow the 6-step guided tour on the dashboard
5. Tap your **initial (top-right)** → **Health Data Settings** → **Sync Health Data** ← Critical!
6. **Apple Watch users:** Open the app on both devices, then tap **Sync Now**

## Daily Tasks

| Task | Time | Required |
|------|------|----------|
| Sleep Log | ~3 min | Yes |
| Assessment | ~5 min | Yes |
| Check-ins | 30 sec x3 | Optional |

**Important:** Complete the Sleep Log from memory — don't check your wearable first. The gap between perception and reality is clinically valuable.
