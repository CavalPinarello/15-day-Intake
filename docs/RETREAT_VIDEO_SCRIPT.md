## ONBOARDING (1:30 - 2:15)

**[Screen recording: Onboarding flow]**

The app walks you through a quick setup:

First, enter your name.

Then set your body metrics — pick metric or imperial, adjust the sliders for height and weight.

Finally, you'll see a HealthKit authorization request — tap Allow. This grants permission for the app to access your health data. The actual sync happens later.

---

## GUIDED TOUR (2:15 - 2:45)

**[Screen recording: Guided tour]**

After onboarding, the app enters tutorial mode — a 6-step guided tour explaining each screen element.

Follow along and tap Next. It explains your three daily tasks: Check-in, Sleep Log, and Assessment.

---

## CRITICAL: SYNC YOUR HEALTH DATA (2:45 - 3:30)

**[Screen recording: Settings → Health Data]**

**[On camera - emphasis]**

This step is critical. Don't skip it.

**[Screen recording]**

Tap your initial in the top-right corner to open Settings.

Under Health Integration, you'll see Apple Health. Tap **Health Data Settings**.

Then tap **Sync Health Data**. This imports 6 months of data from your wearable.

Without this step, we won't have your objective sleep data to compare with your subjective reports.

---

## APPLE WATCH USERS (3:30 - 4:15)

**[Screen recording: Watch app]**

If you have an Apple Watch, the companion app installs automatically.

To sync them: Open Zoe Sleep on your iPhone AND your Watch at the same time. Then in Settings, tap Sync Now.

**[Watch screen recording]**

The Watch app lets you do quick check-ins using the Digital Crown — scroll up or down to rate your energy, mood, and focus. Takes 30 seconds.

**[Watch screen recording: Light Rx]**

It also tracks light exposure. Morning sunlight before 10 AM is the strongest driver of circadian rhythm. Aim for 30 minutes. Check your progress throughout the day.

---

## DAILY ROUTINE (4:15 - 5:15)

**[Screen recording: Sleep Log]**

Every morning, you'll do two things:

First, the Sleep Log. This takes about 3 minutes. It's based on the Stanford Sleep Log — questions about when you went to bed, how long it took to fall asleep, how you slept.

**[On camera - important]**

Answer from your subjective memory. Do NOT check your Apple Watch or Oura first. The gap between how you perceive your sleep and what actually happened is clinically valuable. If you peek at your data first, you contaminate that.

**[Screen recording: Assessment]**

Second, the Assessment. About 5 minutes. Days 1-5 are core questions. Days 6-10 adapt based on your answers — if you mention insomnia, shift work, snoring, you'll get specialized questionnaires.

After you finish both, the app locks until 4 AM the next day. This ensures data quality.

---

Quick tour of Settings:

**Personal Information** — review your profile

**Color Theme** — Circadian mode adjusts colors by time of day

**Sleep Science Cards** — toggle educational cards during assessments

**Debug Mode** — experimental features for the curious, not fully tested
